Online DM access to the terminal, followed by guided ATS setup. The ATS adapter
is bundled with exact browser/context dependencies; the paid ATS engine and
Agent Browser runtime remain separate prerequisites.

publication, production deployment, live account qualification or autonomous
trading. Local fixtures and packed-install checks must remain distinct from
